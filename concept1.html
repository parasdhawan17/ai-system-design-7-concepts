Concept 1 of 7

RAG — Give the Model the Right Knowledge

Retrieval-Augmented Generation. The model does not know your data. You fetch the right data and give it to the model at answer time.

THE BIG IDEA

The goal: right knowledge, at answer time

An LLM knows only its training data. It does not know your company files. It does not know data from today.

RAG fixes this. First you search your documents. Then you give the best pieces to the model. The model answers using those pieces.

Simple ruleGood retrieval = good context = good answer. Bad retrieval = bad answer, even with a great model.
HOW IT WORKS

The RAG pipeline, step by step

  1. Load: Collect documents: PDFs, web pages, tickets, manuals.
  2. Chunk: Split documents into small pieces. Each piece is a chunk.
  3. Embed: Turn each chunk into numbers (an embedding). Similar meanings get similar numbers.
  4. Store: Save chunks and embeddings in a vector database.
  5. Retrieve: Embed the user question. Find the chunks with the closest meaning. This is top-k retrieval.
  6. Rerank: Score the found chunks again, with a stricter model. Keep only the best.
  7. Generate: Send the question plus the best chunks to the LLM. Ask it to answer from the chunks and cite them.
KEY DETAILS

What decides RAG quality

ChoiceWhy it matters
Chunk sizeToo small: the chunk loses meaning. Too big: the chunk adds noise and cost.
OverlapChunks share some text at the edges. Ideas are not cut in half.
MetadataTags like date, product, and source let you filter the search.
Embedding modelIt decides what “similar” means. A weak model finds weak matches.
Hybrid searchMix keyword search (exact words) with vector search (meaning). Each covers the other’s misses.
FreshnessUpdate the index when documents change. Old chunks give old answers.
COMMON FAILURES

Four ways RAG breaks

  • Wrong or irrelevant documents are retrieved.
  • Poor chunking cuts the key fact away from its context.
  • Too much context confuses the LLM. More is not always better.
  • The model hallucinates even with the correct context in front of it.
Interview line“When a RAG answer is wrong, I debug retrieval first, not the prompt. Most RAG bugs are retrieval bugs.”

Quick quiz

  1. Why can a RAG system give a wrong answer even when the correct document exists in the database?
Show answer

Bad retrieval or bad chunking can fetch the wrong pieces. Too much context can also confuse the model. And the model can still hallucinate. Retrieval quality is the limit.
